V Verified reference answer
AA24-109A reports Akira threat actors using Rclone, with advisory mappings Tl 048; AA25-071A
reports Medusa actors using Rclone, with advisory mappings Tl 567.002. These mappings describe
the cited reports and do not establish that every use of the software has these behaviors. The
Akira report cites T 1048 (Exfiltration Over Alternative Protocol); the Medusa report cites Tl 567.002
(Exfiltration to Cloud Storage). These are distinct source-reported mappings for the same
software.
