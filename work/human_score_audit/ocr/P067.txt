Question
According to AA23-075A, which ATT&CK technique is connected to LockBit 3.0 affiliates through
the reported use of rclone? Explain the two-hop actor-to-software-to-technique path with
evidence.
Displayed answer
LockBit 3.0 affiliates use rclone to connect to a remote access software. [E6, E5, E4]
The ATT&CK technique connected to LockBit 3.0 affiliates through the reported use of rclone is
T 1219. [E5, E4]
The two-hop actor-to-software-to-technique path is: LockBit 3.0 affiliates -> rclone -> T 1219.
