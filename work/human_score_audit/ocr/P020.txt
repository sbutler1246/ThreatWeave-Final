What is the two-hop relationship from LockBit affiliates through Advanced Internet Protocol (IP)
Scanner to an ATT&CK technique in this advisory? Explain both the software-use link and the
technique mapping.
Displayed answer
LockBit affiliates use Advanced Port Scanner to scan target networks. [E 10, Ell, E 12, El 3]
Advanced Port Scanner performs network scans, finding open TCP and UDP ports for
exploitation. [El, E2, E3]
Network Service Discovery (T 1046) is the ATT&CK technique used by LockBit affiliates. [E4, E5]
Network Segmentation (MI 030) is the mitigation for Network Service Discovery (T 1046). [E4,
E5]
Network Segmentation (MI 030) involves ensuring proper network segmentation to protect
critical servers and devices. [E4, E5]

V Verified reference answer
The advisory lists Advanced Internet Protocol (IP) Scanner among tools used by LockBit affiliates
and associates its repurposed use with T 1046 (Network Service Discovery in the frozen Enterprise
ATT&CK 17.1 reference). This describes the advisory mapping, not all uses of that tool.
