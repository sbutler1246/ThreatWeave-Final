V Verified reference answer
AA23-352A reports Play ransomware actors using Mimikatz, with advisory mappings T 1003;
AA25-071A reports Medusa actors using Mimikatz, with advisory mappings T 1003.001. These
mappings describe the cited reports and do not establish that every use of the software has these
behaviors. The first report uses the parent technique T 1003 (OS Credential Dumping); the Medusa
report specifies T 1003.001 (LSASS Memory), a sub-technique of T 1003. This is a difference in
specificity of the reported mapping.
