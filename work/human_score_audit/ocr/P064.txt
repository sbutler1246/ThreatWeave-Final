V Verified reference answer
AA24-109A reports Akira threat actors using Mimikatz, with advisory mappings T 1003; AA25-071A
reports Medusa actors using Mimikatz, with advisory mappings T 1003.001. These mappings
describe the cited reports and do not establish that every use of the software has these behaviors.
The first report uses the parent technique T 1003 (OS Credential Dumping); the Medusa report
specifies T 1003.001 (LSASS Memory), a sub-technique of Tl 003. This is a difference in specificity
of the reported mapping.
